Changing a condition resets the simulation and updates the question. The target height controls the word problem, Jump button, and where the balloon automatically stops. It must be between 0 and the initial height. For a realistic classroom scenario, initial height is limited to 100–2,000 ft and horizontal distance to 50–3,000 ft. Values outside these ranges are adjusted automatically. The vertical rate must be negative because the balloon is descending.

Related Rates Mathematics

tan(θ) = y/x
sec²(θ) · dθ/dt = (1/x) · dy/dt
dθ/dt = x/(x² + y²) · dy/dt

The derivative uses radians. Since dy/dt < 0, dθ/dt is negative: the angle of elevation decreases as the balloon descends.

    Simulation Assumptions

    This simulation assumes ideal conditions: no wind, no turbulence, and a constant vertical descent speed. The balloon moves straight down, the observer stays still, and the horizontal distance remains constant. Real balloons may move differently.
